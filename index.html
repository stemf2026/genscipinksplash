The Project

From DIY to PinkSplash

Originally a simple DIY dishwashing liquid, PinkSplash was reimagined by Group 2 STEM-F to be safer, more effective, and more enjoyable to use — while staying eco-conscious.

Original Product

DIY dishwashing liquid focused on creating lather and cleaning dishes. Limited in safety, sustainability, and user experience.

The Goal

Create a modified product that is safer, more sustainable, eco-friendly, visually appealing, and better performing in real dishwashing tasks.

PinkSplash

Improved formula with SLES, CAPB, citric acid, glycerin, salt, pink colorant, and watermelon fragrance — packaged in a convenient pump dispenser.

Why PinkSplash

Key Improvements

Superior Grease Removal

Enhanced surfactants (SLES + CAPB) lift cooking oil and food residue more effectively for cleaner dishes with less effort.

Richer, Longer-Lasting Foam

Foam boosters and viscosity modifiers produce denser lather that stays longer during washing.

Skin-Friendlier Formula

Glycerin acts as a humectant to help retain moisture and reduce dryness after dishwashing.

pH Balanced & Stable

Citric acid helps balance pH, softens hard water minerals, and supports product stability.

Delightful Experience

Attractive pink color and refreshing watermelon fragrance make dishwashing more pleasant.

Convenient Dispenser

Pump-style dish soap dispenser for controlled dispensing and better everyday usability.

What's Inside

Key Ingredients & Functions

Ingredient Function
Sodium Laureth Sulfate (SLES) Primary surfactant — lifts grease, oil, and dirt; produces effective cleaning foam
CAPB (Cocamidopropyl Betaine) Foam booster & mildness enhancer — denser lather, gentler on skin
Citric Acid pH balancer & water softener — improves cleaning in hard water and product stability
Glycerin Humectant — helps retain skin moisture and bind ingredients
Salt (Sodium Chloride) Viscosity modifier — thickens the formula and stabilizes foam
Pink Colorant Visual appeal — gives the signature PinkSplash look
Watermelon Fragrance Oil Pleasant scent — makes the dishwashing experience more enjoyable
